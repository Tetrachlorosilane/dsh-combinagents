# Live Team/task-board verification — 2026-11-19

> This is the pre-hardening live proof. The same-day follow-up added Team
> revision publishing, explicit `team` scope/`team_list`, graph-structure
> guards, continuable-only membership, UI member filtering, and owner
> reassignment; see `docs/DESIGN-REVIEW-2026-11-19.md`. Re-run live checks after
> the Host restart before treating this file as coverage of those changes.

## Scope

Live Host: DSH `0.2.2-rc.5`, profile `desktop`, local GUI
`http://127.0.0.1:60912`, bridge `/dsh-combinagents`, durable storage.

The active root composition had four nodes (root + three children). No Teams or
tasks were present before the test.

## Calls exercised

1. `POST /create-team` with `rootNodeId` at the composition root and
   `nodeIds` naming the root plus `agent-2` (a connected two-node subset).
2. `team_task_create` with subject, description, and `write_scopes`.
3. `team_task_list` and `team_task_get` for the new task.
4. `team_task_update` with `action=reassign`, `owner=agent-2`, and
   `expected_revision`.
5. `team_task_update` with `action=complete` and the next revision.
6. `composition_task` created a composition-only task.
7. `team_task_list` returned only the Team task; the composition-only task did
   not appear in Team scope.
8. `POST /delete-team`; its scoped Team task disappeared with the Team.
9. A direct `POST /update-task` removed the composition-only task.
10. Final `/state` showed `teams: []` and `tasks: []`.

## Results

- Team creation accepted the connected root + `agent-2` subset and persisted
  `nodeIds`.
- Team task output used the native-shaped fields (`id`, `revision`, `subject`,
  `description`, `status`, `owner`, `blockedBy`, `writeScopes`, `ready`,
  `writeScopeWarnings`).
- Reassign produced `owner: "agent-2"` and `status: "in_progress"`.
- Complete produced `status: "completed"`.
- Team and composition task boards remained isolated.
- Team deletion removed its scoped task and left composition nodes/Sessions
  untouched.
- No residual test Teams or tasks remained after cleanup.
