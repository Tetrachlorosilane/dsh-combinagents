# Post-hardening live tool check — 2026-11-20

## Environment

- DSH `0.2.2-rc.5`, `desktop` profile, current local GUI/bridge.
- Durable `combinagents` storage.
- A four-node live composition was already present; no Teams or tasks were
  present before the check.

## Actual Team/task calls

1. Created two Teams through the bridge:
   - **Live Scope A**: root + `agent-2` (connected two-node set);
   - **Live Scope B**: `agent-3` only.
2. Verified the `.revision` token changed after each Team creation.
3. Created a task with `team_task_create` and no `team`: it was scoped to the
   caller's Team A.
4. Created a task with `team_task_create` and `team: "Live Scope B"`: it was
   scoped to Team B, from the composition root.
5. `team_task_list` without `team` returned only Team A's task;
   `team_task_list` with Team B returned only Team B's task.
6. `team_list` returned both Teams with ids, labels, Leads, and members.
7. `team_task_update` with Team B and `owner: "lead"` resolved the Team Lead
   (`agent-3`) and changed the task to `in_progress` with that owner.
8. `team_task_list` with Team B and `owner: "lead"` returned the task.
9. A duplicate Team label was rejected with `TEAM_LABEL_CONFLICT` (HTTP 400).
10. `composition_task` list returned no Team tasks.
11. Cleaned up by deleting both Teams and all test tasks; final `/state` showed
    `teams: []`, `tasks: []`, and the original four composition edges.

## Findings from the live run

- Team revision publishing, explicit `team` scope, `team_list`, Team Lead owner
  resolution, and Team isolation all worked on the live Host.
- A real tool-output bug was found: `composition_task` **create/update** still
  returned Team tasks in its render output even though its list was filtered.
  The action output now filters to tasks without `teamId`, with a regression
  test. HMR reloaded the Host code, and a second live `composition_task` create
  returned **only** the composition task. The test task was then deleted; final
  `/state` was empty for Teams and tasks.
- The structural graph guard could not be exercised live because the affected
  root agent was running (`AGENT_BUSY`); it is covered by manager tests
  (`TEAM_STRUCTURE_CONFLICT`, cross-composition rollback).
